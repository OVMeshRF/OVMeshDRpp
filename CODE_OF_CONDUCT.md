# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique ideas and evidence without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Respect consent around RF collection and sharing survey data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful public material and restrict participation when needed, while considering context and giving a clear explanation where appropriate. Do not reproduce private information while moderating it.

For a sensitive conduct concern, ask the maintainer to arrange a private reporting channel without posting names or incident details publicly. A dedicated confidential conduct-reporting address and response-time commitment are not yet established. Security defects follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
