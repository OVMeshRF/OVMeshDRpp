# RF survey capability and evidence gaps

This engineering assessment describes current capabilities and the evidence still needed for regional RF surveys. It is not an instrument certification or a promise that every survey requirement is satisfied.

## Readiness assessment

The application is a useful **experimental survey and analysis tool**. Its strongest foundation is retained, frequency-resolved RF measurement with explicit gaps, original receiver-position association, reproducible filters and manageable reports. These are more useful for surveying than packet counts alone. Existing test coverage and known limitations are summarized in [validation scope](quality-and-validation.md). Field evidence remains limited. The application is not a calibrated instrument, exhaustive mesh inventory or field-qualified regional channel-selection system.

The narrative report makes existing measurements easier to interpret; it does not remove their measurement or validation limits. Implementation presence, passing synthetic checks and field acceptance are separate statuses.

## What an experienced surveyor is likely to ask

| Area | Available now | Gap and practical consequence | Priority / acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frequency occupancy | SDR fixed-threshold FFT activity per bin, exact selected-bin busy-time union, exposure and known gaps | Threshold is uncalibrated dBFS. Receiver/antenna/gain differences can change what counts as activity | **Before regional comparisons:** calibrated signal-level sweeps across frequency and gain, documented detection probability/false-alarm behavior and reference setup |
| Concentrator sampling | One/two RAK5146 boards collect complete RSSI histograms at assigned scan centers and receive configured LoRa profiles | Revisit gaps, nominal 234.3 kHz filter, uncalibrated RSSI; no continuous occupancy, blind preset search or measured packet width | **Before regional comparisons:** characterize scan timing/sampling bias, receiver offsets, sensitivity, motion and endurance; keep sampled statistics separate from SDR occupancy |
| Channel widths | Arbitrary interval selection, retained fine bins, experimental inferred LoRa BW/SF | Energy span is not configured modem BW or a measured 99% occupied/emission bandwidth. No field-validated census of all channel widths | **Before width/identity claims:** independent known-signal tests at different strengths, offsets, widths and overlapping signals; report edge/resolution uncertainty |
| Time and representativeness | Stored UTC anchor, elapsed time, exposure, known gaps and selectable time groups | No rolling busy-hour analysis, occupancy confidence interval, minimum representative observation plan or reliable packet access-delay estimate | **Before planning claims:** repeat sites over relevant times/days; choose an estimator that accounts for correlated samples and nonstationary traffic |
| Receiver metrology | Gains, signed Offset, sample rate, FFT/Hann ENBW, threshold, descriptions, clipping and center diagnostic | No traceable power/frequency calibration, antenna/cable response correction, validated passband, dynamic-range or uncertainty budget | **Before quantitative power claims:** compare to a calibrated source/instrument and measure linearity, adjacent-channel rejection and analog overload |
| Transmitter power/model | SDR dBFS, RAK nominal RSSI in dBm, and relative comparisons with stable setup | Cannot infer transmitter watts, RAK model, EIRP or excessive power from received strength. Near weak transmitters can look stronger than distant powerful ones | **Outside current survey inference:** controlled calibrated conducted/radiated test with known path/setup; assess authorization separately |
| GPS and maps | Original fixes, fixed/mobile display, missing-position exposure, spatial filters and approximate geographic CSV cells | Indoor drift, sparse visits, tile-end attribution and time/location confounding; no offline basemap, surveyed accuracy or multi-route fusion | **Before regional coverage claims:** repeated routes, stationary checks/reference positions, exposure-by-area review and multi-session comparison |
| Protocol attribution | Generic energy, experimental LoRa waveform observations, legacy Meshtastic key-based decode results | No automatic discovered-waveform payload dispatch; incomplete PHY success; MeshCore, recipient PKI and reliable LoRaWAN/other-device attribution are missing | **Before mesh/non-mesh inventory claims:** labeled independent captures across supported parameter ranges; measure precision/recall and error stages |
| Collision/interference analysis | Overlapping energy and CRC failures may be observed | Neither proves a packet collision or harmful interference; no validated per-protocol airtime subtraction | **Later specialized work:** synchronized independent receivers/known transmissions and controlled interference tests |
| Regional comparison | One source recording with frequency/time/area filters | No multi-session merge/comparison, receiver-calibration normalization or repeat-visit reconciliation | **High next product priority:** compare preserved source sessions with explicit setup and coverage compatibility |
| Reporting | Narrative HTML, frequency/time/geographic CSV, separate waveform/GPS/content data, source ID/schema/settings and privacy choices | No signed provenance chain/source digest in the report, interactive frequency-by-time heatmap, formal uncertainty or automated recommendation model | **Before external decision packages:** review sample reports with regional operators; define versioned shared methodology and evidence requirements |
| Reliability/platform support | HackRF/RTL-SDR and experimental one/two-board RAK adapters, native regression suite, private/exclusive files, bounded workers and bounded receiver evidence | Receiver interruptions and discovery queue loss have occurred; no qualified long mobile/overnight runs, Windows/Linux acceptance or broad RTL tuner-model qualification | **Before broad rollout:** sustained and interruption/recovery tests, disk/resource limits, separate receiver-backend qualification and native platform matrix |

These priorities deliberately keep recording/reporting ahead of automatic channel recommendations. A frequency that looked quiet on one receiver at one time is a candidate for further study, not a proven safe assignment.

## Standards cross-check

ITU-R [SM.2256-2 (June 2026)](https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-r/opb/rep/R-REP-SM.2256-2-2026-PDF-E.pdf), especially sections 3, 6, 7, 9 and 10, discusses detector/selectivity, threshold, timing, presentation, uncertainty and interpretation. The report distinguishes channel occupancy from frequency-band/resource occupancy and discusses geographic averaging. This reference informs the review categories; the specific gaps above describe application behavior and validation scope. No ITU method conformance is claimed.

HackRF's [RX gain documentation](https://hackrf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/setting_gain.html) describes multiple adjustable analog receive stages. That supports recording setup with every comparison; it does not supply a calibrated dBFS-to-dBm conversion for this application. Received power cannot by itself establish unknown transmitter power or identity.

## Narrative report behavior and validation

The following occupancy/report details describe SDR recordings. RAK schema-7 reports instead summarize sampled RSSI histograms, per-board configured reception and health, with optional receiver positions. They do not manufacture busy-time or FFT coverage. See [concentrator reporting semantics](../design/concentrator-measurements.md).

**Generate analysis report...** in Analyze captures the latest completed selection, then allows full-session reset and local destination/privacy review. After reception stops, an asynchronous worker writes a new private `.html` file using a consistent read-only snapshot. It shares the existing CSV aggregation code and introduces no dependency, network request, script or message-content export. The HTML is bounded to 16 MiB; aggregation retains existing limits and fails explicitly rather than silently discarding later source data.

The report includes complete selected frequency-bin rows, peak-preserving frequency visualization, selected-scope metrics, ten highest-occupancy time groups, optional twenty receiver cells ordered by their busiest bin, grouped waveform evidence, selected legacy reception/decode counts and whole-session health counters clearly labeled as such. Limited lists disclose their scope and point to full CSV categories. Defaults exclude coordinates, free-form titles/notes/descriptions and all message contents. Opt-in text is HTML-escaped; a restrictive Content Security Policy disallows active content and external resources. Printing/PDF uses the user's browser.

Report fixtures check alternating versus simultaneous bin activity (100% versus 50% any-bin occupancy, both 50% mean frequency-time occupancy), source immutability, selection/no-exposure behavior, location opt-in, HTML escaping, missing-content defaults, overwrite refusal and removal of incomplete output. Previously recorded targeted test and synthetic browser-render results support those bounded cases; they are not a new test run of this source snapshot.

The report does not supply calibrated RF accuracy, exhaustive protocol attribution, a complete geographic survey, or a reliable estimate of unknown transmitter power. Its summaries preserve measurement limits rather than resolving them. See [validation scope](quality-and-validation.md), [measurement definitions](../design/spectrum-measurement-record.md) and [survey methodology](../design/survey-and-analysis.md).
